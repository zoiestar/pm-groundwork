# Competitive Analysis

**Filename slug:** `competitive-analysis`

## Questions

**Q1 — Who are the 2-5 competitors to analyze?** Freeform list.

**Q2 — What dimensions matter most?**
- Features & functionality → side-by-side comparison
- Pricing & packaging → how they charge, what's included
- Market positioning → brand, messaging, target segment
- User experience → ease of use, design, onboarding
- All of the above → comprehensive

**Q3 — What is your current positioning?** Freeform. Pre-fill from CONTEXT.md.

**Q4 — Internal or external?**
- Internal strategy → candid, detailed, includes your own weaknesses
- External / investor-facing → polished, positioning-focused
- Team alignment → shared with cross-functional partners

## Output sections

Executive Summary · Market Overview · Competitor Profiles (per competitor: overview, strengths, weaknesses, target market, pricing) · Feature Comparison Matrix · Positioning Map · Opportunities & Threats · Strategic Recommendations · Sources

## Note

Never invent competitor facts, pricing, or feature claims. Anything not supplied by the user or found in the workspace gets marked `[needs verification]`.
