# Communication Plan

**Filename slug:** `communication-plan`

## Questions

Pre-fill stakeholders from MEMORY.md and channels from USER.md before asking.

**Q1 — "I found these stakeholders: [list]. Anyone to add or remove for this plan?"** Freeform, or "this covers it".

**Q2 — What cadences exist or are needed?**
- Daily standups → quick sync, blockers, priorities
- Weekly status → progress, risks, decisions needed
- Biweekly review → deeper dive
- Monthly executive update → high-level for leadership
- All of the above

**Q3 — What channels do you use?** Pre-fill from USER.md tools.

**Q4 — Sensitive topics requiring restricted distribution?**
- Yes, let me describe → freeform follow-up
- No → standard distribution
