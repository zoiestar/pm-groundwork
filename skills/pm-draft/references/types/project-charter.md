# Project Charter

**Filename slug:** `project-charter`

## Questions

**Q1 — Who is the project sponsor?** Freeform. Pre-fill from MEMORY.md stakeholders.

**Q2 — What is the business case? Why now?** Freeform.

**Q3 — What are the high-level deliverables?** Freeform. Pre-fill from `PLAN.md`.

**Q4 — What are the known constraints?**
- Budget → fixed or limited, specify if known
- Timeline → hard deadline or time-boxed
- Resources → limited team size or skill gaps
- Multiple → describe all that apply
- None identified yet → include a section to fill in

**Q5 — What decision-making authority does the PM have?**
- Full authority within scope → PM decides within defined boundaries
- Recommend, sponsor decides → PM proposes, sponsor approves
- Shared with steering committee → decisions go through governance
- Not yet defined → include a governance section to clarify

## Output sections

Project Title & ID · Sponsor · Project Manager · Purpose & Justification · Objectives (SMART) · Scope (in-scope / out-of-scope table) · Key Deliverables · Milestones & Timeline · Stakeholders (table: Name, Role, Responsibility, Influence) · Budget Summary · Constraints & Assumptions · Risks (table: Risk, Probability, Impact, Mitigation) · Governance & Decision Authority · Approval Signatures

## Pre-fill

MEMORY.md stakeholders and DECISIONS.md for decisions already made.
