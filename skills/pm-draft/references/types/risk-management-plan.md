# Risk Management Plan

**Filename slug:** `risk-management-plan`

## Questions

Pre-fill risks from the MEMORY.md "Open risks and blockers" section first, show them, then ask.

**Q1 — "I found these risks in your workspace: [list]. Any others to include?"** Freeform, or "these cover it".

**Q2 — What's the risk tolerance for this project?**
- Low → regulatory, safety-critical, high-stakes; mitigate aggressively
- Medium → standard business risk; balance mitigation with progress
- High → innovative or experimental; accept more risk for speed

**Q3 — Who owns risk escalation?** Freeform. Pre-fill from MEMORY.md stakeholders.

**Q4 — How often should risks be reviewed?**
- Weekly → fast-moving, frequent changes
- Biweekly → moderate pace
- Monthly → stable project
- Per milestone → review at each checkpoint

## Output sections

Purpose & Approach · Risk Tolerance Statement · Risk Identification Methods · Risk Register (table: ID, Risk, Category, Probability, Impact, Risk Score, Owner, Mitigation, Contingency, Status) · Risk Assessment Matrix (5x5 probability vs. impact) · Risk Response Strategies by category · Monitoring & Review Process · Escalation Procedures · Risk Communication Plan
