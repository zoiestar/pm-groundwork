# Lessons Learned

**Filename slug:** `lessons-learned`

## Questions

**Q1 — What phase, milestone, or project is this retrospective for?** Freeform. Pre-fill from a recently completed phase in `PLAN.md`.

**Q2 — What went well?** Freeform. Push for specifics — "the team communicated well" is not a lesson.

**Q3 — What didn't go well?** Freeform, same standard.

**Q4 — What would you do differently next time?** Freeform.

**Q5 — Specific recommendations to highlight?**
- Yes, let me describe → freeform follow-up
- Just capture the themes → synthesize from the answers above

## Output sections

Header (project, phase, date, participants) · Executive Summary · Context (what was being done, timeline, team) · What Went Well (categorized: process, people, tools, technical) · What Didn't Go Well (same categories) · Root Cause Analysis for major issues · Recommendations (table: Recommendation, Category, Priority, Owner, Target Date) · Action Items (table: Action, Owner, Due Date, Status) · Appendix: Raw Feedback

## Pre-fill

Cross-reference DECISIONS.md — which decisions turned out right, which turned out wrong? That is the most useful content in the document and the reason the log exists.
